# MindCare business scope

MindCare is a self-care and mental-health tracking platform for end users. It supports emotion journals,
screening assessments, reviewed self-care content, health signals and an AI assistant grounded in approved
sources. It supports awareness and early guidance; it does not diagnose, treat or replace emergency services.

## In scope

- User accounts, privacy controls and notification preferences.
- Daily emotional check-ins, trends and screening history.
- Reviewed self-care articles and practices, including bookmarks.
- AI guidance with citations, feedback and safety guardrails.
- Health Connect/HealthKit ingestion when the mobile integration is merged.
- Clear crisis guidance to trusted people, local healthcare and emergency services.

## Out of scope

- Expert accounts, expert verification or professional profiles.
- Appointment scheduling, consultations, expert chat or clinical notes.
- Consultation payments, refunds or insurance workflows.
- Diagnosis, treatment plans, prescriptions or emergency dispatch.

Product copy, navigation, APIs and future use cases must not promise an internal expert or appointment workflow.
